# NerdQAxe / NerdOctaxe wordmarks

`nerdqaxe-plusplus-wordmark.png` and `nerdoctaxe-wordmark.png` are cut
from the boot screens of NerdOS
([shufps/ESP-Miner-NerdQAxePlus](https://github.com/shufps/ESP-Miner-NerdQAxePlus),
`main/displays/images/themes/{NerdQaxePlus2,NerdOctaxeGamma}/Raw Images/initscreen2.png`,
GPLv3; the artwork is shufps's, who agreed in
[issue #710](https://github.com/shufps/ESP-Miner-NerdQAxePlus/issues/710)).
"NerdQAxe++" and "NerdOctaxe" are trade marks registered by
solomining.de (Bolt Engineering GmbH) on behalf of OSMU; they agreed by
e-mail on 2026-09-29 that WarpAxe shows the names and logos on the boot
screen of these boards. `tools/make-splash.py` turns them into display
sprites.
